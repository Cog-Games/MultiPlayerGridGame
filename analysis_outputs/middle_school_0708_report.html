Key Findings

  • Condition balance was close: 15 participants in A and 17 in B.
  • After deduplicating human-human phone copies, stag capture occurred in 11 of 71 analyzable rounds (15.5%).
  • Most rounds ended with a rabbit: 58 of 71 rounds (81.7%).
  • 3 participant file(s) had fewer than 3 rounds, so completion rates should be read with that data-quality note.
  • 1 participant file(s) had a condition/match mismatch, most likely from a fallback or interrupted matching state.
  • The partner-guess fields were not present in these workbooks, so belief/accuracy analysis is not available for this dataset.
