# Committed Agent: Methods / Model

The committed agent was modeled as a joint-goal inference policy in the `2P3G` task. Before a new goal appeared, the agent followed the baseline joint-RL policy while maintaining a posterior over possible joint goals:

$$
P_t(g)\propto P_{t-1}(g)\,
\pi_{\mathrm{joint}}^1(a_t^1\mid x_t^1,x_t^2,\{g\})\,
\pi_{\mathrm{joint}}^2(a_t^2\mid x_t^2,x_t^1,\{g\}).
$$

After a shared goal had been detected and a new goal was presented, the agent sampled a joint goal at each step according to:

$$
W_\lambda(g)=
\frac{\exp(\beta EU(g))P_t(g)^\lambda}
{\sum_{g'}\exp(\beta EU(g'))P_t(g')^\lambda}.
$$

Joint utility was negative total Manhattan distance:

$$
EU(g)=-(d(x_t^1,g)+d(x_t^2,g)).
$$

After sampling $g_t$, movement was generated by the focal agent's component of the joint-RL policy restricted to that goal:

$$
a_t^i\sim \pi_{\mathrm{joint}}^i(\cdot\mid x_t^i,x_t^j,\{g_t\}).
$$

The sampled goal was recomputed at every post-new-goal decision step. We fixed $\beta=3.0$ and used $\lambda=0.125$ for the committed-agent simulations.
