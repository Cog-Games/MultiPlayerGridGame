sampleJointGoal_afterNewGoal

Joint-RL before new-goal; after new-goal, infer posterior and resample a joint goal every step.

beta=3.0, lambda=0.125 fixed/current branch valuelambda: posterior strength; beta fixed at 3.0

Math

Posterior update
\[P_t(g)\propto P_{t-1}(g)\prod_i\pi_{\mathrm{joint}}^i(a_{t-1}^i\mid x_{t-1}^i,x_{t-1}^{-i},\{g\})\]
Post-new-goal joint-goal sampling
\[W_\lambda(g)\propto \exp(\beta EU_t(g))P_t(g)^\lambda\]
Joint utility
\[EU_t(g)=-(d(x_t^1,g)+d(x_t^2,g))\]

Math

Inherited joint-goal sampling
\[W_\lambda(g)\propto \exp(\beta EU_t(g))P_t(g)^\lambda\]

\[a_{\mathrm{leg}}(g^*)=\arg\max_{a\in\mathcal A^+(g^*)}P(g^*\mid a)\]
Mixture signaling policy
\[\pi_{\mathrm{signal}}(a\mid g^*)=(1-p)\pi_{\mathrm{committed}}(a\mid g^*)+p\,\delta_{a_{\mathrm{leg}}(g^*)}(a)\]

TwoStageSignalAgent_sigmoidThreshold

Starts flexible under joint-RL, monitors joint-goal posterior, then smoothly gates into posterior-weighted committed signaling.

beta=3.0, k=10, tau=2/3 fixed, eta=0 fixed, lambda=0.10 and mixture p=0.00 from success-weighted lambda-p searchlambda: posterior strength; p: mixture probability of taking the legible signal action; tau: gate midpoint; eta fixed to 0 in primary row

Math

Confidence
\[C_t=\max_g P_t(g)\]
Sigmoid threshold gate
\[\rho_t=\sigma(k(C_t-\tau))\]
Committed goal weights
\[W_\lambda(g)\propto \exp(\beta EU_t(g))P_t(g)^\lambda\]
Mixture signaling policy
\[\pi_{\mathrm{signal}}(a\mid g^*)=(1-p)\pi_{\mathrm{committed}}(a\mid g^*)+p\,\delta_{a_{\mathrm{leg}}(g^*)}(a)\]
Blended action policy
\[\pi(a)=(1-\rho_t)\pi_{\mathrm{joint}}(a\mid s_t,\mathcal G_t)+\rho_t\sum_g W_\lambda(g)\pi_{\mathrm{signal}}(a\mid g)\]
