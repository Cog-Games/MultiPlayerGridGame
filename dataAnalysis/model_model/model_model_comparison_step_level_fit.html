sampleJointGoal_afterNewGoal

Joint-RL before new-goal; after new-goal, infer posterior and resample a joint goal every step.

beta=3.0, lambda=18.1 from post-new-goal step-level action likelihoodfit target: step-level human action likelihood

Core Math

\[W_\lambda(g)\propto \exp(3\,EU_t(g))P_t(g)^\lambda\]
\[\pi(a_t)=\sum_g W_\lambda(g)\pi_{\mathrm{joint}}(a_t\mid s_t,\{g\})\]
