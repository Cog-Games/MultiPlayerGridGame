# AlwaysCommittedAgent - Model

## Overview

`AlwaysCommittedAgent` is an always-on joint-goal commitment model. Unlike the main `CommittedAgent`, it does not wait for new-goal presentation or first shared-goal detection before using committed goal selection. From the beginning of the trial, it maintains a posterior over candidate joint goals, samples a joint goal every step, and acts with a joint-RL policy conditioned on that sampled goal.

## Posterior Over Joint Goals

The agent maintains:

$$
P_t(g)
$$

At trial start:

$$
P_0(g)=\frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{G}_0\rvert}
$$

After observing previous actions:

$$
P_t(g)\propto P_{t-1}(g)
\prod_i
\pi_{\text{joint}}(a^i_{t-1}\mid s^i_{t-1},s^{-i}_{t-1},g)
$$

where $\pi_{\text{joint}}(\cdot\mid s,g)$ is the joint-RL action distribution conditioned on one candidate joint goal.

When a new goal appears, the new goal receives mass:

$$
P_t(g_{\text{new}})=\frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{G}_t\rvert}
$$

and old goals are rescaled to preserve relative odds:

$$
P_t(g_{\text{old}})
=
\left(1-\frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{G}_t\rvert}\right)
\frac{P_{t^-}(g_{\text{old}})}
{\sum_{g'\in\mathcal{G}_{t^-}}P_{t^-}(g')}
$$

## Goal Selection

At every step, the agent samples a joint goal:

$$
g_t^{*}\sim W_\lambda(g)
$$

with:

$$
W_\lambda(g)
\propto
\exp(\beta EU_t(g))P_t(g)^\lambda
$$

and:

$$
EU_t(g)=-
\left[
d(s_t^{self},g)+d(s_t^{other},g)
\right]
$$

## Action Policy

After sampling $g_t^{*}$, movement is generated by joint-RL restricted to that single joint goal:

$$
a_t\sim \pi_{\text{joint}}(a\mid s_t,g_t^{*})
$$

In code this uses:

```js
RLAgent.getJointRLAction(selfPos, otherPos, [goal])
```

## Parameters

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\lambda$ | Strength of posterior in goal selection |
| $\beta$ | Inverse temperature on joint-distance utility |

Default runtime settings:

$$
\beta=3.0
$$

The trial-level commitment fit used in the current analyses selected small $\lambda$ values when matching human-human commitment.
